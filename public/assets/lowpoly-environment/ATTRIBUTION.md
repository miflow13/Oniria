# LowPoly Environment Pack attribution

The outdoor library uses selected meshes from **LowPoly Environment Pack** by **k0rveen**.

- Source: https://k0rveen.itch.io/lowpoly-environment-pack
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original pack: 31 low-poly outdoor environment meshes.

Selected assets currently included in this branch:
- Terrain_1.fbx
- Terrain_2.fbx
- Tree_1.fbx
- Tree_3.fbx
- Bush_1.fbx
- Rock_1.fbx
- Mounting_3.fbx
- Log_1.fbx

The meshes are loaded directly by the Three.js outdoor-library scene and reused with scale, rotation, and placement variation.
